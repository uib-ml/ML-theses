Learning in reinforcement learning requires the ability of collecting a reward after taking an action. However, in the real-world, rewards are often not available in real-time and they might be provided only in aggregate form once a certain condition or a milestone has been achieved. This leads to the study of reinforcement learning with bagged rewards [1].

This project will consider different ways in which rewards might be aggregated and presented to the learner. It might consider requirements on the aggregation that still guarantee optimal learning and/or develop algorithmic solutions for dealing with bagged rewards.
