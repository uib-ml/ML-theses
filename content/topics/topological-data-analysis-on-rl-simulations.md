Complex systems and dynamics may be hard to formalize in a closed form, and they can often be better studied through simulations. Reinforcement learning systems, for instance, may be reproduced by instantiating agents whose interactions with the environment and other agents generate complex and emergent dynamics. Still, analyzing the behaviours arising from these interactions, and the connected risks, is not trivial.  

In this project we will consider the use of topological data analysis for categorizing and understanding the behaviour of agents in simulations [1]. We will analyze the insights and the limitations of exisiting algorithms, as well as consider what dynamical or safety information may be glimpsed through such an analysis.
