Graph-based data assimilation, such as non-linear/non-Gaussian methods in triangular measure transport [1] or highly-scalable linear graph-based methods [2], obtains improved inference, uncertainty quantification, scalability, and explainability in ensemble based data assimilation domains. The explainability aspect is vital for users of data assimilation software such as ERT [3] or PEST [4], but is much less explored. How can graphs and conditional independence help users understand which subset of multimodal data that is conditioned on influences the state, and vice versa, how do changes in the prior state influence predictions of multimodal data?
