This is a follow-up project of an [earlier Master thesis](https://hdl.handle.net/11250/3074601) that introduced and studied empirical stability in the context of tree-based models. The goal of this project is to develop stable update methods for deep learning models. You will design several stable methods and empirically compare them (in terms of loss and stability) with a baseline and with one another.
