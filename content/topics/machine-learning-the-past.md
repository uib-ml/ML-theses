The history department at UiB has an ongoing inter-disciplinary research project that aims at developing and validating novel methods for historical research that utilize machine learning. The project aims to address a significant gap in Norwegian history: the understudied intermediary role of the king's lieutenants (lensherrer) between the Danish crown and the local Norwegian society. Given that the main source of data for this research comes from historical handwritten documents, there is an urgent need for machine learning models that can reliably process images of historical handwritten texts and output quality text-based data that can then be utilized for further analysis and research.

The aim of this project will be to study the use of multi-modal models for transforming images of handwritten historical texts into text-based data that can be stored for further processing and research. The primary source of data will be legal and administrative texts written in 16th–19th century Norwegian/Danish, so beside the normal hurdles in HTR from capturing text from images, the model will need to handle things like archaic spelling and scribal variations. Optionally the project could also include studies of machine translation of old 16th–19th century Norwegian/Danish to modern language.
