This is a follow-up project of an [earlier Master thesis](https://hdl.handle.net/11250/3090624) that developed a novel method for learning Acquisition Functions in Bayesian Optimization through the use of Reinforcement Learning. The goal of this project is to further generalize this method (more general input, learned cost-functions) and apply it to hyperparameter optimization for neural networks.
