Simulations are widely used in the real-world to predict the evolution of systems and assess the outcome of policies. However, realistic simulations like digital twins are often expensive to run. On the other hand, computational simulation might be interpreted as the execution of a causal model.

This project will evaluate methodologies to exploit the theory of causal inference to augment the results of simulations by deriving the causal model underlying a program and relying on causal theory to evaluate analytically the outcome of policies.
