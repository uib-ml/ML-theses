Real-world systems can often be studied using causal models representing a system at different levels of resolution. Such a multi-level representation allows us to assess a system from multiple perspectives and to perform computations using the most computationally-efficient representations. However, in order to succesfully use multiple causal models, it is necessary to guarantee the consistency of the causal representations.

This project will aim at looking at different metrics to evaluate the consistency of multiple causal models, comparing them on empirical scenarios, and analyzing the insights they might provide.
