Perhaps the original data-science question in the geosciences is the following: "what rock is it?" In subsurface exploration, wells are drilled and from well logs an abundance of data is obtained. Much of such data is public and available, or can be made available through collaboration with industry partners. Based on various multi-modal data, with new data-science and AI techniques, and from prior knowledge of geological periods and their sequence, benchmarks can be improved. What is important and informative to predict optimal sequences of rock types? Collaboration with industry domain experts will likely be beneficial.
