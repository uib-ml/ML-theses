Divisive covers are a divisive technique for generating filtered simplicial complexes. They originally used a naive way of dividing data into a cover. In this project, you will explore different methods of dividing space, based on principal component analysis, support vector machines and k-means clustering. In addition, you will explore methods of using divisive covers for classification. This project will be mostly computational.
