In topological data analysis, one-parameter filtrations like the Vietoris–Rips complex are overly sensitive to outliers. A more robust approach is to add a density parameter to the filtration, leading to multiparameter persistence.

Rolle and Scoccola [1] recently developed a stable clustering method using the two-parameter filtration degree-Rips, showcasing one way multiparameter persistence can be applied to machine learning.

This project will combine the work of Rolle and Scoccola with our recent work on the Core bifiltration [2], a two-parameter filtration that is both stable and fast to compute for lower dimensions. The goal is to obtain a robust density-based clustering method that is fast to compute.
