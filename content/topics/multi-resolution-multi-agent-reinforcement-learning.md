Real-world system are often composed by multiple agents interacting with each other. Reinforcement learning and game theory study how these interactions may converge to equilibria, often under the assumption that the agents share the same representation of the environment. However, in reality, agents often have different types of sensors and observe the environment at different resolutions.  

This project will explore empirically the dynamics of interaction among agents as their resolutions vary, and it will inquire the conditions under which such shifts might affect the convergence to fixed solutions.
