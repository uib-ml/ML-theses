Higher-Order Message Passing (HOMP) is a paradigm for learning from different generalizations of graphs, such as hypergraphs, simplicial complexes, and cell complexes [1]. They are typically slow to train. You will therefore attempt to train HOMP models from scratch using ideas from low-rank adaption [2]. The goal is to check if this makes training feasible for larger datasets and how it affects predictive performance.
